# System monitoring and auditing: case records, tools and subordinate assistance

2 November 2026 · Documentary revision 1.0.

This chapter defines monitoring and auditing requirements for the Intelligent Cybersecurity Specialised Agent. Actual enablement is recorded for each installation through its accepted coverage, configuration and limitations. This specification does not itself establish continuous monitoring, active prevention or acceptance of all described capabilities.

## Authority and scope

The operator is the authorised person using the Intelligent Cybersecurity Specialised Agent. The **Defender Profile (PD)** identifies the artificial intelligence (AI) model's role, subordinate to the operator and the SV's deterministic governance. It is neither an administrative account nor the complete specialised agent. Required monitoring and preservation must continue when the model is disconnected or the dashboard is closed; continuity is verified during acceptance.

The agent distinguishes its own operating scope from an external scope subject to authorised intervention. The former includes explicitly assigned resources; the latter identifies the owner, resources, permitted operations and engagement authority. A knowledge domain, an administrative domain and a network are different objects. Knowledge does not grant authority over infrastructure. The [common scope and access definition](https://github.com/juantoniolloretegea/SVperitus-dataset/blob/main/agentes/ALCANCE-Y-ACCESO.md) remains authoritative.

Cross-domain security monitoring may protect Immunology and other resources under SV governance. It does not create reciprocal access to case records, documents, credentials or conversations. A model receives only specifically authorised content. Administrative functions, security observation and intervention retain separate permissions.

## Case records and traceability

Each operation is linked to a stable case record identifying its internal or external scope and its purpose: monitoring, preventive auditing or incident response. The record identifies authoriser, executor, reason, owner, technical boundaries, validity, stop conditions, tool versions, admitted sources, processed data and evidence destination. Scope extensions are recorded before use; reachability does not establish authority.

Authorisation, execution, observation and outcome remain distinct. Evidence identifies the resource, source, event and acquisition times, integrity and relationship to the original. A process identifier is distinguished from a particular process creation instance. Losses, duplicates, attribution conflicts and unobserved periods remain visible.

An external assessment establishes which monitoring exists and since when. A tool's current presence does not establish its operation during an earlier incident. Third-party records retain their provenance. Missing records do not establish absence of activity or a satisfactory result.

## Shared acquisition and evidence contract

Acquisition integrates operating-system, process, communication and hardware-reader sources whose coverage has been verified. It may use separate processes to reduce privileges. Examination instruments and the panel read this shared capability under permissions delimited by resource, purpose and period. An exceptional sampling frequency or a new source requires explicit configuration and verification; it must not silently change another operation.

Each stream retains a minimum evidence contract:

- Resource, scope, source, collector version and effective configuration.
- Boot identity and, for processes, identifier and creation time.
- Event, acquisition and reception times, time zone and synchronisation quality; sequence and monotonic time within a run, where available.
- Data type, unit, effective frequency, sampling, aggregation, filters, queue capacity and known or unknown losses.
- Direct or third-party provenance, transformations and links to preserved originals.
- Integrity, access, retention, rotation, backup coverage and recovery conditions.

Chained hashes support comparison against a preserved reference; they do not establish authenticity if the same actor can rewrite both records and references. Independent custody is received according to risk and scope. Telemetry may contain personal data: acquisition, access and export are limited to what is necessary.

System metrics, traces and model usage may be correlated through documented identities and clocks. Usage from a shared account, the whole computer or another task is not attributed to a candidate without specific evidence. Original values and units are retained; the ternary value belongs to the defined judgement and does not replace measurement.

## Preventive auditing and incident response

A preventive audit checks inventory, safeguards, permissions, observation, retention and recovery; it records improvements and verifies authorised changes. It does not presume an incident. Incident response delimits the relevant period and preserves originals; new acquisition remains distinct from historical evidence. A tool installed today does not prove that it was operating when the events occurred.

Conclusions distinguish observations, third-party statements, justified inferences and matters that cannot be established. A gap may have an identified cause —an unconfigured source, permissions, sampling, rotation, loss or interruption— or remain unresolved. **Forensic dynamics** means analysis of the admitted library and evidence with verifiable provenance; it adds no model mode and does not itself establish expert or judicial validity.

Direct inspection, active discovery, installation of a collector and configuration changes are distinct operations and must fall within the authorisation. External work may combine direct acquisition and supplied documentation without merging their provenance.

## Library, established knowledge and research

A documented and admitted vulnerability or incident belongs in the appropriate library edition, with provenance, version, applicability and review. Subsequent use of that knowledge need not remain classified as new research. Publication of an advisory is not admission: identity, integrity, relevance, permissions and its relationship to constituted Universes still require verification.

The **Passive Procedure (P1)** uses previously admitted library material and evidence without browsing; transport through an application programming interface (API) requires separate authorisation. The **Active Procedure (P2)** allows research only within authorised scope and keeps findings separate until admission. The accelerated protocol changes only previously approved configurable conditions, with recorded justification and validity; isolation, integrity, authorisation and nucleus protection remain mandatory. The complete definition remains in [Library and procedures](https://github.com/juantoniolloretegea/SV-motor/blob/main/laboratorio/ensayo-ia-y-observabilidad/biblioteca-y-procedimientos.md).

Conventional and artificial intelligence (AI) threat catalogues are versioned knowledge sources. They are neither exhaustive inventories nor automatic authorisations to execute described techniques.

Delimiting admitted sources does not erase the model's prior knowledge or establish that it has no other knowledge. Acceptance checks delivered material, sources used and procedural compliance; it does not presume isolation of training knowledge.

## Tools and execution boundaries

The tool inventory records purpose, version, licence, verified origin, privileges, effective configuration, authorised resources and accepted coverage. It distinguishes presence, actual use, acceptance and pending capabilities. Selection follows specific needs for processes, communications, web applications, authentication or evidence, rather than installation of an entire distribution.

Third-party tools may observe or intervene through bounded contracts. SV governance, authorisation, admissibility, constituted interpretation and independent stopping remain in its Rust implementation. Tool and model outputs are untrusted inputs: they cannot alter controls or execute as instructions. Rust implementation alone does not guarantee security.

Virtual machines help delimit environments and recover them; they establish neither isolation against every technique nor physical hardware coverage. Network tests establish permitted and prohibited crossings, exposed services and cleanup of owned resources. Boot-time protection retains [its separate specification and acceptance](https://github.com/juantoniolloretegea/SVperitus-dataset/blob/main/agentes/ciberseguridad-inteligente/PROTECCION-DESDE-EL-ARRANQUE.md).

## Routes, evidence and representation

Universes, mandatory dependencies and routes are reused when constituted for the operation. Pending routes remain pending; they do not receive a fictitious U. The vector contains valuations of explicitly defined questions, and the polygon represents the same state, identity and order without replacing evidence.

Each position exposes the question, rule, source, critical condition, value and supporting verification. Model explanation, its own ternary pronouncement and independent verification remain distinct. A vendor score, an alert or absence of alerts does not automatically become 0, 1 or U. [Parameters, routes and evidence](https://documentos-sv.itvia.online/dominios/universos/parametros-rutas.html) and [Consequences of omission](https://github.com/juantoniolloretegea/SV-motor/blob/main/laboratorio/ensayo-ia-y-observabilidad/modelos-de-ia/estandar-comparativo-o-benchmark/CONSECUENCIAS-Y-FUENTES.md) apply.

## Presentation and acceptance

The host page must provide scope and case identity, source health, authorised processes and communications, chronology, library, tools and related cells. It distinguishes operation, observation loss, incidents and pending capabilities. Read access does not enable configuration or intervention, and existing agent work remains accessible.

Acceptance requires verifiable continuity without a model, effective permissions, consumer isolation, checked acquisition, loss detection, preservation and recovery, selective action, deadlines and independent stopping. Partial acceptance establishes only its stated scope. An examination consumes the common sources it needs; complete institutional deployment does not become a prerequisite for every examination parameter by analogy.

Legal preparation identifies applicable rules, responsible parties, purpose, retention and notification routes. Technical traceability assists review but does not itself establish legal compliance, certification or forensic admissibility.

## Reference framework and related reading

Applicability depends on the institution, services, data and jurisdiction. Relevant instruments include the [Network and Information Systems Security Directive (NIS2)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOUE-L-2022-81963), the [General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOUE-L-2016-80807) and, where applicable, Spain's [National Security Framework (ENS)](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/rd/2022/05/03/311/con). Transposition, competent authorities and applicable deadlines must be checked before actual operations; incident notifications remain distinct from personal-data-breach notifications.

As technical references, the [United States National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), publication 800-115](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/115/final), addresses technical assessment planning; [publication 800-61, revision 3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final), addresses incident response. Consulting them does not certify compliance or make recommendations identical obligations for every institution.

The operating guide is available in the authorised Manager workspace. This chapter retains common requirements; scope, procedures, routes and custody remain defined by their linked sources.
